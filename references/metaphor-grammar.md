# Metaphor grammar

Choose one primary operation. A supporting operation may refine it but must not create a second puzzle.

| Operation | Construction | Best use |
|---|---|---|
| Shared contour | Two subjects share one outline | identity, kinship, double lives |
| Substitution | One object replaces a body part or structural element | obsession, loss, transformation |
| Scale reversal | A tiny figure meets an immense object or force | fate, awe, oppression, childhood |
| Negative-space reveal | Empty space forms a second image | absence, memory, hidden truth |
| Containment | A story world appears inside an object, wound, glyph, or silhouette | history, interiority, inheritance |
| Fracture | Meaning occurs across a crack, cut, tear, or missing part | trauma, repair, social rupture |
| Mirror/inversion | Two worlds oppose across a reflective axis | reality/dream, past/present, truth/lie |
| Repetition/anomaly | Repeated forms contain one decisive difference | conformity, investigation, time |
| Material transmutation | Smoke becomes ink, blood becomes flowers, rain becomes text | poetic causality, memory, violence |
| Title-as-architecture | Letterforms become landscape, passage, figure, or obstruction | identity, historical scale, journeys |
| Temporal fold | One space contains different ages or moments | memory, regret, cyclical time |
| Near/far reversal | Close inspection and thumbnail reading reveal different subjects | mystery, dual meaning |

## Construction rules

1. State the literal source of every element.
2. State the conceptual relationship in one sentence.
3. Ensure the relationship becomes visible without explanatory copy.
4. Design the far read first: one silhouette, mass, or contrast.
5. Add the near read: texture, embedded figure, crack, or secondary meaning.
6. Remove any element that does not change the meaning.

## Originality check

- Reverse-search mentally against famous poster structures and avoid recognizable replicas.
- Do not reproduce the exact motif, placement, palette, and typography combination of a reference.
- Translate references into principles, then change subject, spatial logic, material logic, and visual mechanism.
- If a concept depends on naming an artist to make sense, it is not yet a usable concept.
